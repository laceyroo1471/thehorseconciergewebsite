About
the Institute

The Institute for Human-Animal Connection sits in the University of Denver Graduate School of Social Work. Their equine behavior work treats the horse as a horse: cognition, communication, stress, and the effect people have in the barn, on the trail, and in any program that includes horses.

This week’s five lessons come from that coursework. Each weekday video ends with something to do — on Denver’s site, or in the app with the horse you actually have.

    Daily schedule

    October 19–25
    2026

    Five lessons from the Institute for Human-Animal Connection, one each weekday. Watch the video, then do the call to action for that day. Across the week, track two rides or hand walks and write down the behavior you saw while you were with your horse.

    Days stay locked until they open. After Sunday, the full week remains available so you can catch up. Catch-up work still earns grand-prize points. It does not change this week’s prize standings after Sunday at 11:59 p.m. Eastern.

    Monday October 19

    University of Denver and the Questions Worth Asking

    Start with the Institute’s own course page, then look at the rest of their human-animal interaction programs and name the one you would actually take.

    Content unlocks Monday, October 19.

    Today’s lesson

    Equine Behavior course · 5 pts

    Open the Institute’s Equine Behavior course. It is a 15-week online class on how horses learn, how people affect them, and how that shows up in care, handling, and training. Stay signed in so these 5 points log when you go.

    Visit the Equine Behavior course · 5 pts

    Human-animal interaction programs · 5 pts

    Look through the Institute’s programs in human-animal interaction — equine, canine, sheltering, teaching, and the social-work certificate. Come back and name the one that interests you most, and why.

    Browse Learning Programs

    Tuesday October 20

    A Belief You Are Willing to Explore

    Name one belief about horses you are willing to look at again. Then give it two milestones you can actually work this week.

    Content unlocks Tuesday, October 20.

    Today’s lesson

    Journey · 15 points

    In the app, start a journey for that belief. Name the journey for the belief itself. Add two milestones you can work through before Sunday — each one needs a title. Fifteen points score when a journey started this week has two milestone titles.

    A journey you already had in the app does not count. This one starts this week, tied to the belief you just chose.

    Wednesday October 21

    How Is Your Horse Today?

    Before you explain the behavior, notice the horse in front of you. The three smileys are enough.

    Content unlocks Wednesday, October 21.

    Today’s lesson

    Horse profile · 5 points

    Open your horse’s profile and use the three-smiley check-in: Great, Normal, or Off. Five points score from a check-in dated this week. You do not mark anything here.

    Thursday October 22

    How Your Horse Learns

    One observation, written while it is still fresh: what they pick up, what they resist, or what changes when you change one thing.

    Content unlocks Thursday, October 22.

    Today’s lesson

    Quick Update · 5 points

    Add the observation under Quick Update on your horse — type it or use voice. Write how they learn, not a general health note. Five points score when that Quick Update is saved this week with text in the note.

    Friday October 23

    What You Would Tell Them

    Look back at the belief you named on Tuesday. Say what you would tell a friend who holds it — and what you would tell your horse.

    Content unlocks Friday, October 23.

    Today’s lesson

    Looking back · 5 pts

    Restate the belief you were willing to explore. Then write the two sentences the lesson asks for: one for a friend, one for your horse. Stay signed in so these 5 points log automatically.

    Saturday October 24

    Behavior You Can Point To

    Two rides or hand walks this week, each with a note about the behavior you saw while you were together.

    Content unlocks Saturday, October 24.

    Rides and hand walks · 5 points each

    These can happen any day this week, not only today. Track the ride or hand walk with GPS. On each one, add a note about behavior tied to that time together — what they offered, what they avoided, how they came back to you. Five points each, up to two. A track without a note does not score.
