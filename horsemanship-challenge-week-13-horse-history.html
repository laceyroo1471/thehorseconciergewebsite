    Monday November 23

    A Little Update Goes a Long Way

    Make any one update to your horse’s profile or the barn where that horse is kept. 5 points.

    Content unlocks Monday, November 23.

    One update · 5 points

    One change is enough. Open your horse’s profile, or the barn where that horse is kept, and save something that is more current than it was.

    • On the horse’s profile: photo, breed, color, age, gender, height, weight, discipline, or the description.
    • On the barn: where the horse is kept — at home or boarding — or the boarding barn’s name, address, contact, or how many horses are there.

    Keeping information current makes records and summaries more useful. A second edit does not add more points.

    Tuesday November 24

    Put Your Records to Work: Equifacts

    Create Equifacts from the records you have built. Then read it for accuracy. 15 points.

    Content unlocks Tuesday, November 24.

    Equifacts · 15 points

    In the app, generate Equifacts for your horse. It pulls the records you already keep into one overview of the full relevant history: who the horse is, daily management, routine care, the care team, health, and activity — including notes for someone meeting this horse for the first time.

    If you generated Equifacts earlier in the Challenge, generate it again so it uses the records you have now. The 15 points are awarded once, when Equifacts is saved on or after Monday, November 23.

    Then read the result. Equifacts is built only from what is stored. Check that it is accurate, complete, and current, and correct anything that is off before you rely on it.

    Bringing records together helps turn individual entries into a useful overview.

    Thursday November 26

    Create a Summary for a Real-Life Situation

    One more summary from your records, separate from Equifacts. 15 points.

    Content unlocks Thursday, November 26.

    One situational summary · 15 points

    Create one additional summary in the app, separate from Equifacts. Choose a purpose first. Think about who will read it and what that person needs to know.

    • Provider Handoff — for an appointment, or a provider who needs the recent picture. Set the window to the last 12, 18, or 24 months. It gathers the primary concern, long-term history, timeline, current issues, what you have already tried, current management, and questions for the provider.
    • Something’s Wrong — when you want a concern in one place. It uses the last 90 days plus the major history, in that same shape.

    One of those is enough. A second situational summary does not add points, and Equifacts does not count for this 15. If you made one of these earlier, generate it again on or after Monday, November 23, so it uses your current records.

    Before you share it, check the dates, the context, and anything that should be more accurate. These summaries are built only from stored records.

    A useful summary brings together the information relevant to a specific situation.
