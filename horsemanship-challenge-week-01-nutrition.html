About
Mad Barn

Mad Barn is a science-driven equine nutrition company dedicated to improving the health, performance, and longevity of horses through research, education, and evidence-based feeding solutions. Mad Barn develops balanced nutritional products backed by the latest scientific research while providing complimentary nutrition support to horse owners, veterinarians, and industry professionals. Every purchase helps fund ongoing research to advance the field of equine nutrition and make practical, science-based care accessible to the entire equine community.
