How winners are chosen

Highest scores in
each category.

Slate award winners are the highest-scoring eligible provider in each category nationwide. Regional banner winners are the highest-scoring eligible provider in each category within Ohio Valley, North Carolina, and Ocala, FL.

Recognition award recipients are selected from providers who received contest support in multiple categories — reflecting broad trust from horse owners, not a single-category win.

Official rules
  • Contest runs August 1–31, 2026. Only star ratings and written reviews submitted during that window earn contest points.
  • Scoring: 1 point per new star rating, 5 points per new written review.
  • Star ratings may be submitted on a provider’s web profile. Written reviews are submitted in The Horse Concierge app.
  • Providers must have an approved, visible listing in the directory to participate.
  • Slate awards: one overall winner per category (Tack Shop, Veterinarian, Feed Store, Chiro, Trainer, Farrier, Boarding Barn).
  • Regional awards: top provider in each category within the Ohio Valley, North Carolina, and Ocala, FL regions.
  • Recognition awards: twelve ear tag key chains for providers who received contest support across multiple categories.
  • The Horse Concierge team reserves the right to verify listings and exclude entries that appear fraudulent or inconsistent with community standards.
  • Winners will be contacted via the email on file for their listing. Questions: info@thehorseconcierge.com
