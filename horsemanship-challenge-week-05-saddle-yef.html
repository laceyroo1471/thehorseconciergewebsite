Meet
Tasida Kiley

Tasida Kiley is a professional saddle fitter, registered with the Society of Master Saddlers. She serves as a mentor for the North American Saddlery School, and owns Your Expert Fitter, a mobile tack shop and fitting service with 18+ brands — boasting fully custom British-made saddles starting at only $3,500 — with a team of fitters in multiple states and in-house bench repair.

She is also the owner and designer of Famous Saddles, a bespoke luxury line of dressage saddles suited to the biomechanics of today’s modern dressage breeds and the menopausal rider, that has become one of the fastest growing brands among FEI competitors.
