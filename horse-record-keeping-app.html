Keep your horse’s health history, care records, feed and supplements, hoof care, appointments, documents, photos and provider notes organized in one place.

The Horse Concierge gives every horse a living digital record that grows with them — and can be shared with the people who help care for them.

The Horse Concierge is a horse management and record-keeping app for iPhone and Android. It lets horse owners keep health, care, feed, supplement, hoof, appointment, document and provider information associated with individual horse profiles.

It is more than a digital binder. Alongside your horse’s records, you can find trusted equine professionals and build a care team around each horse.

FAQs

Horse record keeping
app FAQs.

What is the best way to keep horse records digitally?

The best way to keep horse records digitally is to keep everything for one horse in one place, rather than spreading it across notebooks, text messages, photo albums and paper files. A good digital horse record keeps health and care history, feed and supplements, hoof and farrier visits, appointments, documents and photos attached to that specific horse, stays available on your phone in the barn, and can be shared with the professionals who care for the horse.

The Horse Concierge provides a centralized horse profile that holds health, care, feed, hoof, document and provider information for each horse you own, so the record travels with the horse instead of living in a drawer.

What horse records can I keep in The Horse Concierge?

Each horse profile can hold health records and care history, injuries, treatments, medications and supplements, feed routines through the Digital Feed Room, hoof photos under Hoof Health, farrier and veterinary visits, appointments and care history, documents and photos, tack and equipment, turnout and exercise logs, GPS-tracked rides and hand walks, and the care team of professionals connected to that horse.

Can I upload my horse’s Coggins and veterinary documents?

Yes. You can upload your horse’s Coggins, veterinary records, registration papers and other documents and photos to that horse’s profile in the app, so they stay with the horse they belong to. You can also type or dictate prior history, and AI helps organize that information into care records rather than leaving you to rebuild years of history by hand.

Can I keep farrier and hoof records in the app?

Yes. You can record farrier visits and see time since the last farrier visit, add your farrier to the horse’s care team, and upload hoof photos under Hoof Health so you can follow changes in the hoof over time. Farrier appointments and notes stay in the same care history as the rest of the horse’s record. If you still need a farrier, you can search the provider directory by ZIP code.

Can I track my horse’s feed and supplements?

Yes, and it goes further than a simple feed note. The Horse Concierge includes a Digital Feed Room where you add the feeds, hay and supplements you actually keep on hand, then assign them to individual horses with the amounts each horse receives. Feed changes and supplement entries are recorded over time, so you can see when a diet changed and what it changed to.

The app also includes hay and feed cost review, so you can understand what your feeding program actually costs. Supplements and medications are tracked alongside the horse’s other health and care records.

Can I add horse records using my voice?

Yes. The Horse Concierge includes Voice Updates, which let you speak instead of typing to create horse notes, care updates, training logs and observations. Voice Updates exist so you can capture information while it is fresh in the barn, rather than waiting until you have time to sit down and type it later.

Can I share my horse’s records with my veterinarian or farrier?

Yes. You can build a care team for each horse by connecting the veterinarians, farriers, trainers, equine dentists, bodyworkers, boarding facilities and other professionals you use — including providers who are not already in the directory. You can also generate Professional Summaries, which are shareable horse summaries that help your care team understand your horse’s history and recent care before an appointment.

Equine professionals can list their services free and connect with the owners they already work with.

Is The Horse Concierge available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. The Horse Concierge is available for iPhone and iPad on the Apple App Store and for Android on Google Play. The app is free to download and offers an optional premium subscription.
