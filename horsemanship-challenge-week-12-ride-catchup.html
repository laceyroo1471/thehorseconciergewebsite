    Monday November 16

    Be Present With Your Horse

    Slow down and spend intentional time with your horse. Watch Shelayne Fico here, visit her learning page, and send her one question. 5 + 5 points.

    Content unlocks Monday, November 16.

    Today’s lesson · Shelayne Fico

    Today is about being with your horse. Shelayne Fico’s lesson, Aromatherapy With Your Horse: Everyday Care and Connection, is a way into slowing down and spending intentional time together.

    While you are with your horse, notice how they respond — where their attention goes, and what changes when you stay a little longer.

    Her learning page · 5 points

    Visit Shelayne’s learning page for more on everyday care and connection. 5 points.

    Visit Shelayne’s learning page · 5 pts

    One question for Shelayne · 5 points

    Submit a question about aromatherapy to Shelayne. 5 points.

    Tuesday November 17

    Pause and Notice

    One GPS hand walk or ride. Pay attention to movement, behavior, and engagement. 10 points for the first outing.

    Content unlocks Tuesday, November 17.

    Take yesterday outside

    Build on Monday by noticing your horse while you move together. Watch movement, behavior, and engagement — how they travel, where their attention is, and how willing the work feels.

    First GPS outing · 10 points

    Record one GPS hand walk or ride in the app. The first recorded GPS outing is 10 points. Friday’s outing is a second one, on a different day. This activity scores only once.

    Wednesday November 18

    See the Whole Horse

    Write one observation after Tuesday’s outing. Look back at the diet and hay you already logged. 5 points.

    Content unlocks Wednesday, November 18.

    What you already have on record

    Connect what you saw in motion with comfort, behavior, the environment, and eating. Notice appetite, how long they spend eating, and whether anything is left behind. Diet and hay are already in the app from earlier weeks — Feed and Supplements for the diet, and the Digital Feed Room for hay. Open those records if you want to look back. You do not need to enter them again.

    One observation · 5 points

    Add one observation in the app after Tuesday’s outing. Describe what you noticed about movement, behavior, or engagement. Everyday care can be part of that note. 5 points for one observation.

    Thursday November 19

    Understand What’s in the Hay

    Read Equine Balanced Support on how to read a forage analysis, then answer one question. 5 + 5 points.

    Content unlocks Thursday, November 19.

    Today’s lesson · Equine Balanced Support

    Yesterday you noticed how this horse eats. Today connects that routine to the forage already on record. Equine Balanced Support explains how to read and interpret a forage analysis. The lesson lives on their site, and it is shared here with their permission.

    The article · 5 points

    Read Equine Balanced Support’s article on their site. 5 points.

    Optional, and not scored: if you already have a hay analysis, review it while you read. If you don’t, sit with one question you would ask your hay provider. You do not need a hay analysis to earn the points.

    Read the Equine Balanced Support article · 5 pts

    One question · 5 points

    Answer from the article. The correct answer is 5 points, once. A wrong answer does not award points, and you will see the right one.

    When comparing two hay analyses, which values does the article recommend using—and why?

    Friday November 20

    Put Observation Into Practice

    A second GPS hand walk or ride, on a different day from the first. Notice what feels consistent, and what feels different. 10 points.

    Content unlocks Friday, November 20.

    A second look

    Go out again. Notice what feels consistent with your first outing, and what feels different — the same willingness, a new tightness, a change in attention.

    Second GPS outing · 10 points

    Record a second GPS hand walk or ride in the app on a different day from the first. 10 points. This has to be a separate outing. The first outing does not score again, and further outings do not add Week 12 points.

    Saturday November 21

    Support Your Care Community

    Share one useful firsthand experience: a provider you have used, or a trail you know. 5 points for one.

    Content unlocks Saturday, November 21.

    Choose one · 5 points

    Thoughtful care includes the people and places you already trust. Complete one of these. 5 points total. Completing another option does not add Week 12 points.

    • Rate or review a provider you have used. Search the directory, then leave the rating or review in the app. Any honest review counts. The points are the same at every star rating.
    • Submit a trail you know.
    • Review a trail you have used.

    A written provider review in the app is for a provider on your horse’s care team. Add them there first if the app asks you to.

    Sunday November 22

    • Something you noticed about your horse.
    • Something you learned.
    • One care priority, or one question, to carry forward.
