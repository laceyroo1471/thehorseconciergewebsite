About
The Equine Institute

The Equine Institute builds veterinary-developed courses for the people who take care of horses. Their aim is to take the intimidation out of professional-level care and leave you confident enough to notice a change and ask a better question.

This week’s lessons come from Equine First Aid Essentials. Monday through Friday cover the ordinary digital pulse, vital signs, how a wound heals, bandage materials, and how two common pain relievers are used. Saturday is the first aid kit: a lesson and a downloadable list. Sunday is the recap.

    Monday October 26

    A Baseline You Can Feel

    Recovery week asked how fast your horse comes back after work. Today’s baseline is the ordinary digital pulse in a quiet foot — the comparison you will want if this horse is ever off.

    Content unlocks Monday, October 26.

    Today’s lesson

    In Recovery week, baseline was a resting heart rate: the number your horse returns toward after similar work. This week, baseline is what a quiet foot feels like on an ordinary day. The Equine Institute shows where to put your fingers, what a stronger digital pulse can mean, and why that check belongs in a routine look at the lower limb. If you found this pulse during hoof week, take it again while the horse is fine and notice what ordinary feels like in this horse.
