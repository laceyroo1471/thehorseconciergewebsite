How it works
Compete
Points & prizes

Earn points through app actions and partner activities — care, safety, rides, and more. Climb for the $2,500+ grand prize pool and weekly giveaways.

Follow along

Not competing? Stay close through email updates and the private Facebook group — same learning, your pace.

Your score updates automatically from Hub and app actions. Grand prize standings (top 20) live on the Prize Hub. Each week’s prize race lives on that week’s page. Weekly winners are announced the following Wednesday, after points are validated.
