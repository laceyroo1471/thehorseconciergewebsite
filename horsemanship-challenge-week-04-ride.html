    Friday September 25

    Know the Right Supplements

    Testing is the most effective way to know what your horse actually needs.

    Content unlocks Friday, September 25.

    How do I know which Vitamin E

    Adding another product to the shelf feels like doing something. The more reliable path is a test — then supplementing for what the results actually show. That is true for every supplement you feed, not only Vitamin E.

    Based on national averages, a trace mineral panel is about $50–$60 and a vitamin panel is about $40–$80. Does it make sense to ask about those tests at your next routine veterinary visit?

    A year of guessing is often more expensive than testing once and targeting what your horse actually needs.

    General education only — not an individual veterinary or nutrition recommendation. Panel prices are national averages and vary by lab and region. Talk with your veterinarian before adding, stopping, or changing any supplement.
