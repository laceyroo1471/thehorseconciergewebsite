About Double D
Trailers

Since 1997, Double D Trailers has been building horse trailers the way they should be built: safer, more comfortable, and made for the real demands of hauling. As a family-owned, factory-direct company, we custom-build every trailer in the USA, giving customers direct input on layout, size, and features suited to their horse, tow vehicle, and lifestyle. Safety is at the core of everything we build, starting with our patented SafeTack® Reverse design and carried through in every detail of the trailer.
